# Writing tells

Phrases and formatting habits that add length without information, or that mark text as machine-written. Each row gives what to write instead.

## Openers and closers

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| "Certainly!", "Sure!", "Great question", "I'd be happy to" | The answer, as the first words. |
| "Let me explain…", "Here's a breakdown of…", "To answer your question…" | The answer. The structure shows that an explanation follows. |
| "Looking at your code…", "Uh oh", "There seems to be a problem" | The symptom: `auth.test.ts fails: expected 200, got 401.` |
| "Let me know if you have questions", "Hope this helps", "Feel free to…" | The `next:` line, or nothing. |
| "So to summarize…", "In summary", a recap section | Nothing. The lead line and headings already summarize. |

## Filler connectives and phrases

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| "Additionally", "Moreover", "Furthermore" | `also`, or start the sentence with its subject. |
| "It's worth noting that X", "It's important to note that X" | X. |
| "Ensure that you run…", "Make sure to run…" | `Run…` |
| "Keep in mind X" | X, placed in the step where it matters. |
| "In order to" | `to` |

## Inflation and staging

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| "pivotal", "crucial", "robust", "seamless", "comprehensive" | The specific property: `retries 3 times`, `covers all 12 endpoints`. |
| "serves as", "stands as", "acts as" | `is` |
| "It's not X, it's Y", "not just X but Y" | Y. |
| "Here's the thing:", "The result?", "The catch:" | The thing, the result, the catch. |
| A trailing clause that grades the sentence ("…, ensuring reliability", "…, highlighting its importance") | End the sentence at the fact. |
| Three parallel items added for rhythm | Only the items that exist. |

## Hedges

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| "perhaps", "possibly", "might", "it seems", "could potentially" | The claim, if you checked it. If you didn't, one first-person marker: `unverified: I didn't run it against prod data.` |
| "should work" | What you ran and what it showed, or `untested`. |

## Formatting

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| `**Label:** text` bullets | A sentence, or a table when the labels repeat across items. |
| Bold scattered through a paragraph | No bold, or bold on the one phrase the reader must not miss. |
| Several em dashes per section, or a line opening with one | Commas, colons, or a new sentence. About one em dash per section at most. |
| Emoji, exclamation marks, horizontal rules between sections | Nothing; headings already separate sections. |
